LIVE names whatever you hold (up to 10 notes). SHOW lets you pick a chord, modifiers, inversion and bass note and lights it on the keys in its simplest voicing, rooted in the middle-C octave; play it and the keys turn mint when you match.

THRU forwards everything from IN to an output. To use the keyboard in FL Studio at the same time, create a loopMIDI port, pick it here as THRU, and in FL enable that port instead of the keyboard.

SCORE writes what you play onto manuscript. Notes snap to the grid at the metronome's tempo. With the metronome running, bar lines follow its clicks; without it, your first note is beat 1 and a silent bar closes the phrase. PAGE fills 3 rows of 4 bars and turns the page; SCROLL keeps one big row moving left as you play.

● REC records a session in any mode (one bar of count-in when the metronome runs), ■ stops, and EXPORT saves it as MusicXML, MIDI or PDF. IMPORT opens a MIDI or MusicXML file; ▶ plays it on the keys like a player piano. ROLL shows it as a paper roll.

Computer keys: Z S X D C V… and Q 2 W 3 E R… play two octaves · ← → octave · Space sustain · Esc release all · Enter play / pause. Click the chord name or staff to hear it.
